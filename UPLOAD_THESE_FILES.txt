After deployment:
1. Open /admin
2. Go to SEO
3. Add the KDH company LinkedIn Page URL, Facebook Page URL, and X/Twitter profile URL
4. Click Save SEO Settings

These URLs are stored in content/site.json under the social object and are used as company sameAs structured-data references. Individual lawyers continue to use their own LinkedIn field under Attorneys.
